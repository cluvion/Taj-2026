01 — Executive Summary

The Strategy
at a Glance

This 6-month plan is built for one outcome: qualified international leads who book premium hair transplant procedures with Dr. Aritaj Aiumtrakul (Dr. Taj) at TRIA Medical Wellness Center, Piyavate Hospital, Bangkok.

The strategy is not generic. It is built around Dr. Taj's specific strengths — his surgical precision, Long Hair FUE expertise, and medical educator identity — aimed squarely at high-income patients in Australia and Singapore who would never consider a budget clinic.

The plan operates across three primary platforms: Instagram (fame, reels, lead generation), YouTube (authority, long-form trust), and X/Twitter (medical thought leadership).

"A hair transplant should not look like a hair transplant."

Paid media — Meta/Instagram ads, YouTube pre-roll, WhatsApp click-to-message, and retargeting — drives qualified traffic into a platform-native lead funnel. No website redesign required.

The brand positions Dr. Taj at exactly 60% celebrity surgeon and 40% serious medical educator. Premium patients want a doctor they trust medically and admire personally.

Why the Economics Work

At USD $10,000 per procedure, a single booking recoups roughly one month of total investment. The goal is not viral fame — it is building enough qualified brand authority that the right patients self-select and reach out.

Positioning &

Core Positioning

Brand Feel

Premium, clean, medical, luxury, calm, trustworthy, international, expert. No cheap clinic energy. No discount messaging.

03

60 / 40 Balance

60% celebrity surgeon — visible, aspirational, famous. 40% medical educator — credible, evidence-based, serious.

Tone of Voice

Confident but never arrogant. Educated but never clinical. Personal but never casual. Dr. Taj speaks as a trusted expert who happens to be famous — not a celebrity who happens to be a doctor.

  • Use "patient" not "client"
  • Say "design" not "cut"
  • Say "planning" not "procedure"
  • Never use "life-changing guarantee"
  • Always mention recovery, consultation, and variation

Visual Direction

All content should look like it was shot for a premium international magazine, not a local clinic social account.

  • Consistent palette: deep neutrals, whites, medical clean
  • Cinematic wide shots inside TRIA
  • Dr. Taj on camera: confident, calm, in-frame
  • Before/after: consistent lighting, same angle
  • No stock footage. No generic surgical overlays.

12 — Compliance & Safety

Medical Marketing
Standards

Content Standards

  • No guaranteed results. Always "results may vary."
  • No unrealistic claims. Never "life-changing guarantee."
  • No body-shaming. All content is respectful.
  • Consent-approved before/after only. Written consent required.
  • Consistent before/after: same lighting, angle, background.
  • No surgery footage. No graphic clinical content publicly.
  • Risks, recovery, and variation must be mentioned.

Advertising Standards

  • Australia-facing ads: Avoid testimonials (AHPRA guidelines).
  • Singapore-facing ads: Singapore Medical Council standards.
  • No exaggerated before/after in ads.
  • All ad copy reviewed by Dr. Taj before publication.
  • No competitor attacks. Doctor Reacts is educational only.
  • Celebrity analysis must be legally safe and respectful.
  • WhatsApp communications must include privacy statement.
