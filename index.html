01

Executive
Summary

This 6-month plan is built for one outcome: qualified international leads who book premium hair transplant procedures with Dr. Aritaj Aiumtrakul (Dr. Taj) at TRIA Medical Wellness Center, Piyavate Hospital, Bangkok.

The strategy is not generic. It is built around Dr. Taj's specific strengths — his surgical precision, Long Hair FUE expertise, and medical educator identity — and TRIA's premium hospital-standard environment, aimed squarely at high-income patients in Australia and Singapore who would never consider a budget clinic.

The plan operates across three primary platforms: Instagram (fame, visual trust, reels, lead generation), YouTube (authority, education, patient journey, long-form trust), and X/Twitter (medical thought leadership, professional visibility, and network reach).

"A hair transplant should not look like a hair transplant."

Paid media — Meta/Instagram ads, YouTube pre-roll, WhatsApp click-to-message campaigns, and retargeting — drives qualified traffic into a platform-native lead funnel that requires no website redesign or SEO investment.

The brand strategy positions Dr. Taj at exactly 60% celebrity surgeon and 40% serious medical educator. This balance is intentional: premium patients want a doctor they trust medically and admire personally. The content system, weekly cadence, and campaign architecture are all designed to achieve both simultaneously.

Why the economics work

At USD $10,000 per procedure, a single qualified booking recoups approximately one week of total investment. The goal is not viral fame. The goal is to build enough qualified brand authority that the right patients self-select and reach out — then TRIA's lead team handles the close.

Brand
Strategy

Core Positioning

Bangkok's premium hair restoration surgeon for international patients. Not a clinic. Not a brand. A doctor.

Brand Feel

Premium, clean, medical, luxury, calm, trustworthy, international, expert. No cheap clinic energy. No discount messaging. No hype.

Balance

60% celebrity surgeon — visible, aspirational, famous. 40% medical educator — credible, evidence-based, serious.

Tone of Voice

Confident but never arrogant. Educated but never clinical. Personal but never casual. Premium but never inaccessible.

Dr. Taj speaks as a trusted expert who happens to be famous — not a celebrity who happens to be a doctor.

  • Use "patient" not "client"
  • Say "design" not "cut"
  • Say "planning" not "procedure"
  • Never use the phrase "life-changing guarantee"
  • Never use "transformation" alone — pair it with "carefully planned"
  • Always mention recovery, consultation, and results variation

Visual Direction

All content should look like it was shot for a premium international magazine, not a local clinic social account.

  • Consistent color palette: deep neutrals, whites, medical clean
  • Cinematic wide shots inside TRIA
  • Close-up precision shots: hairline design, surgical planning
  • Dr. Taj on camera: confident, calm, in-frame — not behind slides
  • Natural light where possible; studio-quality for hero content
  • Before/after: consistent lighting, same angle, neutral background
  • No stock footage. No generic surgical overlays.

Compliance &
Safety Guidelines

All content and paid advertising must adhere to the following medical marketing compliance standards.

Content Standards

  • No guaranteed results. Use "results may vary" in all before/after content.
  • No unrealistic claims. Avoid phrases like "life-changing" or "permanent guaranteed".
  • No body-shaming. All content is respectful and dignity-preserving.
  • Consent-approved before/after only. Written consent required for all patient imagery.
  • Consistent before/after standards: same lighting, angle, background where possible.
  • No surgery footage. No graphic or clinical surgical content in any public material.
  • No non-medical influencer medical claims. Influencers may not make procedure claims.
  • Content must be educational and realistic. Risks, recovery, and variation must be mentioned.

Advertising Standards

  • Australia-facing ads: Avoid testimonials per AHPRA advertising guidelines.
  • Singapore-facing ads: Adhere to Singapore Medical Council advertising standards.
  • No exaggerated before/after in ads. Must reflect realistic, documented outcomes.
  • Disclosures on sponsored content where applicable.
  • All ad copy reviewed by Dr. Taj or designated TRIA medical reviewer before publication.
  • No competitor attacks. All "Doctor Reacts" content is educational, not disparaging.
  • Celebrity analysis content must be legally safe, respectful, and opinion-based only.
  • WhatsApp and lead form communications must include privacy statement.
